# Requested feature implementation — v0.3.0

| Requested feature | Implemented behavior | Important scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dedicated Metabolic Tracker tab | Regular/weekend wake, breakfast, lunch and dinner times; selectable weekend days; local reminders; actual check-ins | Planned time never pretends an actual meal occurred. No measured metabolic/insulin response. |
| Automatic weekly diet after onboarding | Seven-day localized general-fitness meal ideas and planning budgets | Medical profiles get an organizer for their existing care-plan meals/targets. No generated therapeutic diet. |
| Country-appropriate food | Nine country selections mapped to local starting cuisine templates; familiar-food field and custom template option | No IP tracking or claim of a complete global recipe database. |
| Vegan versus non-vegan protein | Structured vegan, vegetarian and mixed choices; filtered meal ideas and visible protein sources | No assumption that a vegan diet needs a blanket higher protein target; no nutrient guarantee for unmeasured portions. |
| Snap and warn | Explicit review gate for medical context, allergy checks, dietary conflicts and entered carb-reference overages | No camera diagnosis, guaranteed spike prediction or food-safety certification. Actual food logs remain available. |
| Flexible meal for fit users | Optional choice once per local week for eligible profiles | Disabled for medical contexts; no extra calorie allowance. |
| Steroid awareness ages 18–50 | Dashboard education cards, dedicated screen and optional weekly/daily local messages | Warnings cover non-prescribed anabolic misuse, distinguish prescribed corticosteroids and never provide dosing/cycles/taper instructions. |
| Doctor notice everywhere | “Share this with your doctor. Apply these suggestions as advised by your doctor.” on every screen, in planning notes and routine/education reminder content | No doctor dashboard or automatic transmission to a clinician. |

## Evidence used for educational boundaries

- NHS vegan diet: https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/how-to-eat-a-balanced-diet/the-vegan-diet/
- NIDDK healthy living with diabetes: https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/healthy-living-with-diabetes
- FDA food allergy label review: https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/have-food-allergies-read-label
- Expo 57 local notifications: https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v57.0.0/sdk/notifications/
