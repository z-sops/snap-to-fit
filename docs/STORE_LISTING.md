## Positioning
**The safe way to lose weight — on or off GLP-1.** Snap your meal, protect your muscle, learn the truth about the gym.

Who it is for: US adults (18+) losing weight — including people on GLP-1 medicines (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound) — and beginners who want to train safely.

## Full description (Google 4000 chars; Apple similar)
Snap to Fit is the calorie and fitness app built for real life — and for the millions of Americans on GLP-1 medicines.

SNAP, SCAN OR SEARCH
• Snap a photo: AI finds the foods and portions, then prices each one against USDA reference data where it can — and shows an honest calorie range, not a fake-precise number.
• Scan a barcode on any package.
• Search 8,000+ USDA foods, popular restaurant items and home dishes from American, Mexican and Indian kitchens.
• Asks about cooking oil and butter — the calories most apps miss.

PROTEIN-FIRST, GLP-1 READY
• Daily protein range to help you keep muscle while you lose weight.
• GLP-1 companion: next-dose countdown, shot log with injection-site rotation, side-effect check-ins, and a 30-day summary to share with your doctor.
• Label-based warnings that always point you to your doctor. We never suggest doses or changes.

TRAIN SAFELY
• 870+ exercises with step-by-step form, filtered for your knees, back, neck, shoulders and heart.
• Guided workouts with spoken workout cues, rest timer and set tracking. Your phone reads the cues out loud — one-way audio only; the app never listens to you.
• Gym Truth: the science on supplements, steroids, water pills, soreness and more — no brands, nothing to sell.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• My numbers: BMR, TDEE (learns from your logs), BMI and protein.
• Weekly report, streaks, achievements and weight trend.
• Apple Health and Health Connect (Fitbit) for read-only steps, weight and heart rate. Workout sessions are not imported.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your logs are encrypted on your phone. No ads. Your health data is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Snap to Fit provides general wellness information and is not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before changing your diet, exercise or medicines. In an emergency call your local emergency number (911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK).

## Canada (en-CA) and United Kingdom (en-GB) — same product, local settings
The US listing above is the master. The Canadian and British store listings use the
same feature text with these differences, so the stores are not rejected for
inaccurate descriptions:

| | US | Canada | United Kingdom |
|---|---|---|---|
| Units | lb, ft/in (kg/cm available) | kg, cm by default; lb optional | kg, cm by default; stone and pounds optional |
| Dates | 10/21/2026 | 2026-10-21 | 21/10/2026 |
| Clock | 1:05 PM | 1:05 PM | 13:05 |
| Spoken cues | US English voice | Canadian English voice | British English voice |
| Emergency number in copy | 911 (US) | 911 (Canada) | 999 (UK) |
| Official health sources | FDA, NHS | Health Canada | NHS |

The app language (English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português) is a separate
setting from the market. A user can read the app in English while living in
Toronto or London, and then gets Canadian or British formats and voice.

Canadian and British users are told plainly that the bundled food database is the
US USDA reference set, and that comprehensive Canadian (CNF) and UK (CoFID)
catalogues are not yet included.

## Privacy labels / Data safety (to confirm against the final build)
- Health & fitness data (meals, weight, readings, medicines): collected, stored on device; sent to our server only for features you use (AI photo, coach, backup). Not used for ads. Not sold.
- Photos: meal photo sent for AI analysis only with consent; progress photos stay on device.
- Barcode numbers: sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product.
- No third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
