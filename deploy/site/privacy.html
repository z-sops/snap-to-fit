Privacy policy

This policy explains what Snap to Fit (“we”, “the app”), operated by {{OPERATOR_NAME}}, collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

The short version

  • Your logs are stored encrypted on your phone.
  • We never sell your data and we have no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Data leaves your phone only for features you choose: AI photo and coach, optional account backup, barcode lookup, gym search, CGM connection and subscriptions.
  • You can export and delete your data at any time.

Information stored on your device

Profile (name, age, sex, height, weight, goals, health conditions you choose to enter, medicines, movement limits, diet and allergies), meals, workouts, readings (weight, waist, glucose, blood pressure), GLP-1 dose and side-effect logs, chat history and progress photos. On phones these are kept in an encrypted database. Progress photos are never uploaded.

Information sent when you use a feature

  • AI photo analysis and AI Coach (only after you turn on AI consent): a resized meal photo, or your question plus the profile details needed to answer, are sent to our server and then to Google’s Gemini API to generate a reply. We use a paid Gemini plan, under which Google does not use the content to improve its products. We do not keep meal photos after the reply.
  • Barcode lookup: only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts.
  • Account and backup (optional): username, a salted password hash, recovery-code hash and session hashes; manual backups are encrypted on our server with a server-held key (not end-to-end encryption).
  • Gym search (optional): your one-time location or the area you type is sent through our server to Google Maps. We don’t store it.
  • Dexcom CGM (optional): encrypted authorization tokens are stored on our server to fetch your historical readings.
  • Apple Health / Health Connect (optional): read-only steps, weight and heart rate, used only on your phone.
  • Subscriptions: purchases are handled by Apple or Google; RevenueCat confirms your subscription status.

What we don’t do

No selling or renting of personal data, no advertising, no cross-app tracking, no use of health data for marketing, and no sharing with data brokers. Health information is not used to train AI models.

Consumer health data and breaches

We treat everything you log as consumer health data and share it only as described above or with your consent. If an unauthorized disclosure of your health information ever occurs, we will notify you and the US Federal Trade Commission as required by the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule.

Retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you clear it (Settings → export/clear). Account data and backups stay until you delete your account in the app (Menu → Account and backup → Delete account) or ask us. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days for security.

Your rights

Depending on your state (for example California, Washington, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Nevada), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data and to withdraw consent. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}; we respond within 30 days.

Age

Snap to Fit is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Security

Encryption on the device and in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, encrypted backups and limited server access. No system is perfectly secure.

Changes

We will update this page and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
