Delete your account and data

In the app (fastest)
  1. Open the menu (☰) → Account and backup.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm with your password.

This permanently deletes your account, backups and connected CGM tokens from our server.

Without the app

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from any address with your Snap to Fit username. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Data stored only on your phone is removed when you clear it in Settings or uninstall the app. Store subscriptions must be cancelled separately with Apple or Google.
