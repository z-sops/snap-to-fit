Common questions

Why is AI photo analysis off? Turn on AI consent in Settings. Without it, you can still search, scan barcodes and quick-add.

My barcode isn’t found. Not every product is in Open Food Facts yet. Search by name or use Quick add with the label.

Steps or weight don’t sync. Allow Snap to Fit in Apple Health or Health Connect, and make sure your tracker app shares to them.

How do I cancel a subscription? In the App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
